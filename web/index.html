Help

Open and protect
Drop a photo or choose Open image. Set the parameters and click Protect photo. The first run takes longer while models load. Photos are processed locally as RGB.
Protection methods
PhantomSeal → FaceShield runs PhantomSeal first and applies FaceShield to that result. The combination is experimental and does not guarantee stronger protection. Its changes accumulate; the FaceShield noise limit bounds only the second stage. PhantomSeal steps and FaceShield controls apply only when their method is selected. NullSwap is the PhantomSeal reconstruction of the paper, using a native 256px face crop and a bounded perturbation mapped to the original photo. It has no optimization-step controls. Old presets without a method keep their original FaceShield-only behavior.
NullSwap parameters
NullSwap strength scales its perturbation from 0 to 4; the default is ×1.00. The maximum change is 8 × strength out of 255 before PNG rounding. Strength 0 adds no perturbation. NullSwap seed chooses the initial noise: use a whole number from 0 to 4294967295, with 0 as the default. Reuse a seed for repeatable noise or change it to try another variation. These controls apply only to NullSwap. Higher strength can be more visible and does not guarantee better protection.
Compare
Original and Result show a single photo. Slider uses a draggable divider. Side by side shows both. If output is resized, Original shows the matching resized source for a fair comparison. New runs still use your full original upload.
Zoom and pan
Scroll to zoom. Drag to pan. In Slider mode, drag the divider; hold Space or Shift while dragging to pan. Fit to view or double-click resets the view. Use arrow keys when the divider is focused.
Difference map
Diff shows absolute pixel changes. Black means identical. Gain amplifies changes; RGB shows color differences and individual channels are shown in grayscale. SSIM, PSNR and Changed describe image similarity, not protection strength.
Settings and presets
Settings lets you edit protection parameters and save named presets in this browser. Save settings applies your draft. Cancel, Escape or closing the dialog discards it. Reset restores the recommended defaults.
Export
Export PNG saves the selected photo, or the full-resolution difference map in Diff mode. The exported file keeps the current channel and gain settings. In a combined run, Export PhantomSeal PNG saves the intermediate first stage; the main Result and comparison always show the final output against the clean original.
Test protection
After protecting a photo, Test protection compares real outputs from the selected SimSwap, DiffSwap, InfoSwap or UniFace model for the clean source and available protected stages. First compare the Unprotected swap with Your clean original. Each PNG/JPEG95 baseline must reach the adjustable numeric quality cutoff and receive your visual confirmation before any comparative delta is shown. A score of 0.80 is not 80% confidence, and accepting the baseline is not a successful-defense verdict. Weak baselines are inconclusive; their raw scores and images remain visible. FaceNet and SimSwap also participate in PhantomSeal, so this is a diagnostic comparison, not proof of general protection. The test uses the photo as an identity source and does not evaluate replacing the face inside that photo. Closing the test window keeps the job running; changing the photo, result, test job or cutoff clears visual confirmations.
Protection limits
These methods add changes intended to make some face-swap methods less effective. They do not guarantee protection against every model. JPEG, resizing and editing can weaken the effect. Keep the protected PNG.
